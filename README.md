## Recent work and reusable methods

- **Resumable SharePoint migration:** Discovery → inventory → dependency/permissions analysis → target DMS architecture → provisioning → pilot → automated migration → exception handling → reconciliation → validation → cutover. Azure Automation and PnP PowerShell process approximately 400-file chunks with existing-file checks, metadata preservation, JSON reporting, and final reconciliation logic. Migration and acceptance remain in progress.
- **Power Platform modernization:** Re-architect workflows around Solutions, connection references, managed ownership, environment separation, testing, and production runbooks. Engagement-letter modernization replaces legacy InfoPath forms with SharePoint and Power Apps, integrating Power Automate across approximately 65 fields and eight screens.
- **Business workflows:** Audit Engagement Letters, Company Kudos, SALT Tax intake, and Risk Advisory tracking connect structured SharePoint data to routing, approvals, Teams/email notifications, and assignment/completion processing. Kudos and SALT testing is ongoing.
- **Governance and recovery:** Inventory ownership and permissions, define lifecycle and retention controls, automate remediation, and validate results. Purview retention and preservation patterns complement SharePoint governance and repeatable recovery procedures.
- **Enterprise reporting and operations:** MDA work included Microsoft 365 security-policy automation with PowerShell/Graph, Teams-to-Jira workflows, annual IT assessment reporting through SharePoint/Power Automate/Power BI, and lifecycle replacement forecasting to support planning and budgeting.
- **Copilot and AI:** Ground support answers in approved knowledge, establish identity and retrieval boundaries, connect actions to support escalation, and evaluate realistic scenarios. Additional architecture work includes expertise discovery, employee AI-idea capture, and AI-assisted audit knowledge reuse.
- **Private RAG and MCP architecture:** Normalize knowledge, design semantic chunks and metadata-filtered retrieval, and separate retrieval from controlled tool actions. ## Delivery approach

**Business problem → Discovery → Requirements → Architecture → Security/governance → Implementation and automation → Testing/validation → Results → Operational handoff.**

I establish identity and ownership boundaries early, validate focused pilots, and build monitoring, support, change control, and recovery into delivery. For migrations, that includes dependency analysis, metadata and permission checks, exception reporting, reconciliation, and cutover/rollback planning. For agents and workflows, it includes scenario testing, escalation paths, release controls, and maintainable ownership.
